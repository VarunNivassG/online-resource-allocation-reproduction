# Research smoke run — results and interpretation

This folder was generated by `python scripts/run_research_smoke.py` using `configs/research_smoke.json`.

## Output inventory

- `results.csv`: 172 per-run records.
- `summary.csv`: 86 grouped summaries with mean, standard deviation, and percentile-bootstrap 95% CIs.
- `traces.csv`: 1,850 per-request trace rows for representative instances.
- `plots/`: 15 research figures, including six direct algorithm-comparison plots.

## Exploratory observations from this small run

There are only two seeds in the smoke configuration, so these are pipeline checks rather than paper-level conclusions.

- **Compute–regret:** At T=100 and a cap of 10 LP calls, the adaptive method averaged 7 actual calls and regret about 14.75, compared with 10 calls and regret about 16.70 for the matched fixed-frequency method. At the smaller cap of 5, it averaged 4 calls and regret about 15.79 versus 5 calls and regret about 16.17. With only two seeds, these differences are not conclusive.
- **Change detection:** In the smoke seeds, the resource-shift cases were detected after the known shift point, with average delay around 7 requests at T=100 and around 16–17 at T=50. Reward-only shifts were detected less consistently, which is useful evidence that this detector needs separate calibration for different types of shifts.
- **Risk-aware pricing:** Its effects were mixed. The aggregated smoke results improved regret in the reward-shift and stationary scenarios, but were slightly worse in the resource-shift and gradual-drift scenarios. It reduced potential accepts blocked by the hard feasibility guard in these smoke cases, but this does not establish a general improvement.
- **Resource feasibility:** Maximum measured violation was zero in this run because the online simulation retains its hard feasibility guard. Therefore, violation magnitude is not discriminative in this implementation; the report also includes guard rejections and resource utilization.

## Recommended next experimental pass

Use `configs/research_scale.json` with more seeds, tune the detector threshold and risk weight on a validation split, and preserve separate held-out seeds for final evaluation. Report all seed-level data, actual LP calls, runtime, 95% CIs, change-detection delay, false alarms, reward/regret, and guard rejections. No claim of a formal regret or risk guarantee is made by the included heuristics.
